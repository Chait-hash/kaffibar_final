The Origin Story

Why Kaffibar
Was Born

"I saw young professionals getting sick — not by bad luck, but by bad choices forced by the lack of accessible healthy options."

— Mr. Sumit Kumar, Founder

Mr. Sumit, the founder of Kaffibar, was deeply concerned by the alarming rise in lifestyle-related diseases among young professionals. He realized that unhealthy eating habits and lack of awareness were silently damaging lives across India.

This led to the birth of Kaffibar — a café that combines taste, nutrition, and convenience under one roof. With a focus on protein-packed meals, cold-pressed juices, and gluten-free options, Sumit's mission is to make healthy eating a mainstream choice while building a strong community of health-focused franchise partners across India.

Today, Kaffibar stands as living proof that premium quality and genuine affordability are not opposites — they are the very foundation of a brand built on love, science, and purpose.
